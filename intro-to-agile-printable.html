Lost at Sea

Your yacht caught fire in the Atlantic. The crew is gone, the boat is sinking, and your location is unclear. You have a lifeboat and matches, plus the 15 items below.

Rank every item from 1 (most important) to 15 (least important) for survival while waiting for rescue. Use each rank once.

  • A sextant
  • A small mirror
  • A mosquito net
  • A 25-liter container of drinking water
  • A case of army rations
  • Maps of the Atlantic Ocean
  • A floating seat cushion
  • A 10-liter can of oil and fuel mixture
  • A small transistor radio
  • 2 square meters of opaque plastic tarp
  • A box of shark repellent
  • A bottle of rum (80% alcohol)
  • 500 meters of nylon rope
  • 2 boxes of chocolate bars
  • A fishing kit with rod
